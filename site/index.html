How it works

More Google reviews, without asking twice.

Most happy customers would leave a review. They just don’t want to search for your business, find the right button and type your name. Infinity removes every one of those steps.

  1. Place the card where people pay

    On the billing counter, the table, the reception desk or inside the bill folder.

  2. The customer taps their phone

    No app to download. The phone reads the card and shows a link to open.

  3. Your review page opens

    They pick the stars, write a line and post. The whole thing takes less than a minute.
