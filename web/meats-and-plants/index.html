    Our story

    One table for meat lovers and plant lovers.

    Meats & Plants is a Chef Soham eatery. Japanese-inspired ramen and sushi sit next to multi-cuisine plates and good coffee, so whoever you bring, everyone finds their thing.

    The menu

    What to order

    Come taste it
    • Japanese Chicken Ramen

      Slow-simmered broth, springy noodles, tender chicken, soft egg and nori.

    • Veg Ramen Bowl

      Miso-vegetable broth with tofu, mushrooms and greens. Just as comforting.

    • Crispy Tempura Roll

      Topped with tempura crumbs and a mayo drizzle, served with soy.

    • Cucumber Maki

      Fresh, crunchy cucumber rolled in seasoned rice and nori.

    • Spaghetti Aglio e Olio

      Garlic, olive oil and chilli flakes, tossed through spaghetti.

    • Multi-cuisine specials

      Chinese, continental and comfort plates that change with the season. Ask what is cooking today.

    • Signature Iced Coffee

      Cold and creamy in fluted glass, with a biscuit on top.

    • Café Frappé

      Frothy, strong and cold. Goes with everything.

    • Latte

      Smooth espresso and steamed milk, for slow afternoons.
