What it catches

01Cash shortLitres sold × rate vs cash + UPI + card + credit. Any shortage shows up the moment the shift closes.
02Selling between shiftsEach opening reading must match the last closing on that nozzle. A gap means fuel went out with no one on record.
03Fake readingsA meter photo is required at opening and closing, and the shift starts only from the nozzle's own NFC tag.
04Personal QR paymentsCustomers see the official pump QR. A shift with very low UPI is flagged.
05Short fillingCustomers tap the nozzle tag to check the rate, watch for 0.00 and report problems to the owner, not the staff.
06Stock leaksOpening stock + fuel received − fuel sold vs the dip reading, per tank, every day.
