    Where the evening begins

    The Bar

    Spirit-forward and amber-lit. A clock above the shelves, lamps glowing under the arch, and a seat at the counter for whoever arrives first.

    The Drawing Room

    A grand piano, gilded mirrors and a chequered floor. The first room you encounter, and the one that sets the tone for everything that follows.

    02

    Room two

    The Reading Room

    Soft lamps, arched alcoves, a chandelier and curated books lining the shelves. The most contemplative corner of the residence.

    03

    Room three

    The Assembly Hall

    Lantern chandeliers under a vaulted ceiling and one long table, set for the evening’s most animated conversations.

    04

    Room four

    The Council Chamber

    Private, deliberate, formal. For occasions that call for a room with a sense of history, and the patience to linger.

    05

    Room five

    The Governors Parlour

    Intimate, feminine and refined. Striped walls, gilt portraits and velvet chairs: the most personal room in the residence.

    06

    Room six

    The Archive Room

    Stacked with history, artefacts and the quiet hum of another century. Every object has been chosen; every candle is lit.
