Brew & Bloom Café · Table QR codes

Print these and stick one on each table. Each QR has its own secret code, so the kitchen knows the table and can spot fake orders.

How to try the demo with 2 phones:
1. On phone A, open the kitchen screen (PIN 1234).
2. On phone B, scan a table QR below (or tap a card) and order something.
3. Watch the order arrive in the kitchen, then tap through Confirm → Preparing → Ready → Served and see phone B update live.
